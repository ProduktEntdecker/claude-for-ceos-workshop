# Speaker notes: Claude Code for SME Owners and CEOs

Claude Community House Barcelona, Monday 21 September 2026, 13:00 to 15:00, Room 4.
Matches `slides.html` 1:1. Full prompts, fallbacks and guardrails are in the facilitator guide on the repo.

## Slide 1: Title (13:00)

- Quick show of hands: who uses Claude every week? Who has Claude Code installed?
- One line on the thesis before moving on: today is about judgment, not typing speed.
- Logistics up front: QR code to the repo is already on screen, claude.ai should be logged in, no confidential data goes into any chat today.
- Say once: "If the network fails, I have prepared results, so nothing today depends on the Wi-Fi."

## Slide 2: The thesis (13:02)

- Read the thesis line, then the four patterns are the "why" behind the three formats, not a checklist to dwell on.
- Land on "sparring partner, not a typist": this is the sentence to repeat when something looks like plain task automation.
- Sotarena S.L. gets introduced here as the fictional company behind every demo today.

## Slide 3: The three formats (13:04)

- Say explicitly: no sitting back, every block opens with a short live demo before participants try it themselves.
- One breath per card: triage and briefing, tasks with a hard limit, then a board that disagrees with you on purpose.
- Bridge line: "Ten minutes of me typing, then it is your keyboard."

## Slide 4: The run of the show (13:06)

- Point at the times, not the content: this is orientation, not a new topic.
- Flag the break at 14:00 and the task for it: pick a decision to bring to block 3.
- Note that hands-on work only needs a browser at claude.ai, nothing to install.

## Slide 5: The CEO's morning (13:10)

- Set the scene before switching to the terminal: Monday 07:30, 14 emails, 30 minutes until the management meeting.
- Live in Claude Code: triage, then push back once ("E03 has a deadline today, why is it not act today?"), then the briefing.
- Hand-off line at 13:24: "Your turn, open the claude.ai version, steps 1 to 3."
- Debrief at 13:32: ask where their Claude disagreed with the live run, and why.

## Slide 6: Chief of staff, with trust levels (13:35)

- Frame it first: you trust an AI chief of staff the way you would trust a new employee, read from day one, draft from week one, a short list of acts, nothing else alone.
- Live: notes to task list, then weekly status, then a draft letter marked "DRAFT, NOT SENT".
- Ask for the edit anyway once, out loud, so the room sees the guard block it, not just hears about it.
- Hand-off: write your own whitelist, on paper or in claude.ai.

## Slide 7: The rule underneath (13:39)

- Walk the four bands top to bottom, read level is free, the bottom band never moves.
- Say the two-layer point plainly: instructions ask nicely, a script enforces; in claude.ai only the first layer exists, you are the second.
- This slide can run under the live demo or right after it, whichever keeps the room's eyes up.

## Slide 8: Your advisory board (14:05)

- Why, in one sentence: leaders rarely hear disagreement without an agenda, three lenses do.
- Show the prepared Sotarena chat for 60 to 90 seconds: the questions in round 1, the disagreement in round 2.
- Read the data rule aloud before releasing the room: roles, not names, rounded numbers, nothing under an NDA.
- Walk the room during the 20 minutes of work; at minute 14:29 call time for pairs, 3 minutes each.

## Slide 9: What to try on Monday (14:40)

- Read the three steps as a ladder: triage this week, tasks after the next meeting, the board before the next big call.
- Slow down on the guardrails, this is the slide people photograph.
- Close with: everyone writes one Monday step, two voices share it out loud.

## Slide 10: The repository (14:55)

- Point at the QR code again, it has been on screen since 13:00.
- Closing round: one word each on what they will try on Monday.
- Last line: "The repository stays available, the claude.ai versions need no setup at all."
