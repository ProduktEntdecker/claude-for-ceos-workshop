**Commercial terms (price list 2026, valid from 12.01.2026)**
- **Lead time:** 6 weeks standard for the 12 stock RAL colours, from order confirmation. It was 4 weeks until 31.03.2026, and the target is 4 weeks again by Q2 2027. Custom RAL colours: 8 weeks.
- **Custom RAL:** from 50 pieces, surcharge 12%.
- **Order size and payment:** minimum order value EUR 2,500. Key accounts pay 30 days net. New customers pay a 50% deposit.
- **Warranty:** 5 years on welded frames, 2 years on coating and rope. Spare parts are available for 10 years.
- **Coating spec:** polyester powder, salt-spray tested for 1,000 hours (ISO 9227).

**Value proposition.** Contract-grade outdoor furniture made near Barcelona for hotels, restaurants and beach clubs where salt, sun and 200 guests a day wear furniture out. Sotarena competes with:
- low-cost Asian imports, on durability, spare parts and service
- premium Italian and Spanish design brands, on price, custom colours and delivery within Europe.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Role | CEO since 2017, board member, part of the Vilaró family that owns 70% |
| Age | 52 |
| Background | Industrial engineer from Barcelona. 12 years with an automotive supplier in Germany and France (plant controller, then operations director). Joined Sotarena in 2013 as COO |
| Languages | Catalan, Spanish, English, German, French |
| Style | Numbers first, direct, visible on the shop floor; hates surprises from sales promises |

**Goals**
1. Reach 11% EBITDA by 2027 without losing key accounts.
2. Bring a well-founded coating recommendation to the board on 15.10.2026.
3. Get out of daily firefighting: two protected deep-work blocks per week.
4. Build a management team that decides within its authority without escalating.
5. Prepare the company for a possible change in ownership around 2030 (the Tuset family may sell its 30%).

**Pains**
1. About 140 e-mails a day. The first hour of every morning goes to the inbox.
2. Decisions from meetings are not tracked, so the same topics come back week after week.
3. Knowledge sits in people's heads, as the Quality Manager's retirement showed.
4. Sales promises that operations cannot keep, especially on lead times.
5. The board and the bank want numbers faster than Excel delivers them.
6. Too little time to think; everyone wants her opinion.
